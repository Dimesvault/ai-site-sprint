FAQ

How much does a custom website cost?

Traditional agencies charge $15,000–$30,000 plus monthly retainers. AI Site Sprint builds the same agency-grade custom websites for a one-time flat rate: $2,250 (Bronze, 1–3 pages), $3,450 (Silver, up to 7 pages), or $5,450 (Gold, complex builds with e-commerce or custom apps). No retainers, no hidden fees.

How fast can you build a website?

3 to 7 days depending on tier. Bronze sites go live in 3 days, Silver in 5, and Gold enterprise builds on a 7-day fast-track. AI-accelerated development lets us deliver in days what traditional agencies deliver in months.

AI website builder vs hiring an agency — which is better?

DIY AI website builders are fast but generic; agencies are custom but slow and expensive. AI Site Sprint combines both: human designers and engineers using AI-accelerated workflows to deliver fully custom, conversion-optimized websites at AI speed and a fraction of agency cost.

Do I own my website?

Yes — 100%. You receive full administrative control, all source code, and every account credential on day one. No hostage code, no locked dashboards, no platform lock-in.

What industries do you build websites for?

Every industry — including real estate (IDX/MLS integration), healthcare (HIPAA-conscious booking), e-commerce, B2B SaaS, legal, hospitality, and local services with local-SEO-optimized pages.

Do you offer website maintenance after launch?

Yes. Optional monthly maintenance plans for build clients run $349–$599/month, including updates, security, performance, and 1–3 monthly strategy calls. No contract — cancel anytime.
